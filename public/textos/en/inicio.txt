Scientific exploration leaves much more than discoveries. It also leaves behind machines, instruments, and technologies that made it possible to go further, understand our planet, and explore other worlds.

But what happens to that equipment when their missions end? What discoveries did they make possible? Where are they now? And what mysteries remain unsolved?

NASS is more than an archive: it is a space where you can also be part of the stories left by space exploration.

Here you can discover, learn, and investigate the traces of scientific missions, get to know the legacy of their technologies, share ideas, and get closer to the questions that are still waiting for answers.
